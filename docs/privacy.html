Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MindShift Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the MindShift Productivity YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, manage its playlists, and read the channel's own video statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • The OAuth token is stored privately by the channel owner and used only to act on their own channel.
  • No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the MindShift Productivity YouTube channel's About page.
